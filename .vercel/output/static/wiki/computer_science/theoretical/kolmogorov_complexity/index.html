Kolmogorov complexity

Basic idea

The Kolmogorov complexity K(x)K(x)K(x) of a string xxx is the length of the shortest program (for a fixed universal Turing machine) that outputs xxx. It is the formal notion of “intrinsic information content” — and it is uncomputable.

Complex Numbers

Basic idea

Numbers of the form z=a+biz=a+biz=a+bi with i2=−1i^2=-1i2=−1. They unify rotation and scaling via polar form z=reiθz=re^{i\theta}z=reiθ and make the reals algebraically closed.

Key formulas

  • i2=−1i^2=-1i2=−1, i3=−ii^3=-ii3=−i, i4=1i^4=1i4=1
  • Sum/product: (a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i(a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i(a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i; (a+bi)(c+di)=(ac−bd)+(ad+bc)i(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac−bd)+(ad+bc)i
  • Conjugate: zˉ=a−bi\bar z = a - bizˉ=a−bi; zzˉ=∣z∣2z\bar z=|z|^2zzˉ=∣z∣2
  • Modulus: ∣z∣=a2+b2|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}∣z∣=a2+b2​
  • Argument: arg⁡z=atan2⁡(b,a)\arg z = \operatorname{atan2}(b,a)argz=atan2(b,a)
  • Polar form: z=r(cos⁡θ+isin⁡θ)=reiθz = r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)=re^{i\theta}z=r(cosθ+isinθ)=reiθ
  • Euler’s formula: eiθ=cos⁡θ+isin⁡θe^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\thetaeiθ=cosθ+isinθ; Euler’s identity: eiπ+1=0e^{i\pi}+1=0eiπ+1=0
  • de Moivre: (cos⁡θ+isin⁡θ)n=cos⁡(nθ)+isin⁡(nθ)(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)(cosθ+isinθ)n=cos(nθ)+isin(nθ)

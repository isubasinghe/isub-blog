complex numbers

Basic idea

Extension of the reals by adjoining iii with i2=−1i^2=-1i2=−1. Algebraically closed: every non-constant polynomial has a root.

Key formulas

  • i2=−1i^2=-1i2=−1
  • Euler’s formula: eiθ=cos⁡θ+isin⁡θe^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\thetaeiθ=cosθ+isinθ
  • Modulus: ∣a+bi∣=a2+b2|a+bi|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}∣a+bi∣=a2+b2​
  • Conjugate: a+bi‾=a−bi\overline{a+bi}=a-bia+bi​=a−bi
